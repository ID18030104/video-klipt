## 6. Language
- Composition: one label centred on bottle glass; eye 1 = the arch engraving (three oaks), 2 = brand name, 3 = what’s behind the doors (dates). Slow push-in over 20 s.
- Type: a label printer’s voice. Gloock (sharp high-contrast display, engraved feel) for the name/dates; Instrument Serif italic for the spoken line; Manrope 600 wide-tracked caps for the regulatory small print (AOC, hectares).
- Colour: from the bottle and the vintage: bottle glass #0F1A15, label paper #F1EADA, engraving ink #1D2A22, chenin gold #C29A45 (foil), tuffeau cellar inside #2A2118 with candle warmth #E8C47A.
- Material: laid paper (feTurbulence + chain lines), stroke engraving that draws itself (hatching), gold foil with one light sweep, vine leaf ornaments.
- Brand constant: the name only.

- Stranger in the feed: the open door with "7 & 8 novembre" lands at once; but the doors covered the text edges. Fixed: interior copy set narrower (2-line title and CTA).
- AI detector: the gap between doors read as a black bar and the two door halves drew out of sync (visible seam). Fixed: warm candle light in the seam, per-leaf stagger so the engraving draws as one picture. Gold line collided with the frame rule: rule removed, block moved up.
