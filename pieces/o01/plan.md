# o01 — Relai 2.0 · film hero 16:9, 28 s, FR

**Business/offer**: Relai, SaaS B2B (Série A, 40 pers.). AI agent answering customers 24/7 in 32 languages, inside e-mail/chat/WhatsApp, hands off to a human with full context. Proof: 71 % solved w/o human · 6 h → 40 s · 4,8/5 · 300 clients.
**Objective**: launch + consideration (proof). Runs as homepage hero, LinkedIn (muted autoplay), prospect decks. Rewards: a number in frame 1, sound-off legibility, loopable.
**Viewer**: head of support / scale-up CEO / e-merchant, muted, scanning. Fears: robot that frustrates customers, migration pain. Stops for: a painful number they recognise.
