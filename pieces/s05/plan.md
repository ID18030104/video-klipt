# s05 — Stride Collective · "Night 10K" — plan

Note on assets: the client asked for royalty-free images taken from the internet. Pexels is blocked on this network (curl 22), so everything comes from Open Images (Flickr, CC BY 2.0, credited in meta.json).

## 1. Business & offer
Stride Collective: Berlin run club + running store in Kreuzberg. Mid-price, community-first (the club is the marketing engine for the store). Differentiator: a free, untimed, welcoming run — no pace, no podium — that ends at the shop with a DJ.

## 2. Objective / channel
Event attendance (400 sign-ups) + store footfall (afterparty at the shop). Instagram feed, 4:5. Feed rewards a poster-like image people save/share and a clear single action; it is judged at ~450 px wide in a grid.

## 3. Viewer
Berlin runners and curious beginners, 22–40, thumb-scrolling, sound-off, 1.5 s. They want a reason to run at night in a group; they fear being too slow / judged / going alone. What stops them: a big number, a sunset-to-night image, and the words "free" and "untimed".

## 4. Decisions
- **A. Ambition**: Tier A (event announcement, post). Dials: crafted-editorial / poster-polished with a social-native sticker · energy upbeat · register warm + bold · funnel: conversion (sign-up) · lifespan: campaign (until 22 May). Options considered: B (too plain for a flagship community event), S (over-invests for a single feed post). A it is.
- **B. Structure (reading order)**: Hook (NIGHT 10K on the horizon) -> when (FRI 22 MAY · 21:00) -> what you get (free, untimed, headlamps, afterparty) -> action. Option: offer-blast, teaser. Chosen: event-poster reading order (event trailer logic in one frame).
- **C. Attention mechanics**: (1) bold number at the top — "10K" occupying half the canvas (hook #1); (24) scarcity — round "400 spots" sticker on the runway (payoff/action). Options: question hook (weaker on a poster), face-to-camera (no suitable photo).
- **D. Style family**: 17 Neo-grotesk poster (condensed bold type stacked, colour blocks, info stickers), pushed toward event-poster/airfield signage. Options: 26 Music/club poster (too acid for a beginner-friendly run), 25 Sports performance (clichéd). Reference world: a Swiss/Berlin club-and-sport event poster with runway markings.
- **E. Image treatment**: 6 Duotone/gradient map — one shared map (ink -> dusk violet -> ember -> lamp yellow -> cream) applied to sky, track, disco photo. Options: teal & orange (generic), natural (photos too uneven to sit together).
- **F. Type**: 4 Condensed stack — Big Shoulders Display 900 (industrial airfield signage feel) + Space Grotesk (labels/body). Options: Anton + Inter (default look), Unbounded (too youth-brand).
- **G. Colour**: 10 Photo-derived — sampled from the sunset hero: ink #07061A, dusk #2A1560, ember #E8481F, lamp #FFAA2B, cream #FFF1DC.
- **H. Camera**: static tableau (post); depth via parallax-like layers: sky, ink type on horizon, runway perspective, lamps.
- **I. Transitions**: n/a (still).
- **J. Format**: event poster, 1080x1350.

## 5. Message & action
Message: on 22 May, 400 Berliners run the old Tempelhof runways at night — free, untimed, headlamps included — and party after. Action: sign up via link in bio, stride.berlin.

## 6. Concept
"Run into the dark": the numerals NIGHT 10K stand as ink silhouettes on the last light of the horizon, and the old runway centreline leads straight to them, with a stream of headlamps running toward the sun-down. One dusk grade ties sky, track and dance-floor photos together.

## 7. Layout (1080x1350, 60 px margins)
- y 60–100: header row — STRIDE COLLECTIVE / Berlin · Kreuzberg (Space Grotesk 700, 28–30 px).
- y 130–850: NIGHT / 10K in ink (Big Shoulders 900), standing on the horizon at y≈850, sky photo (sunset) behind, sun glow. Focal point: 10K.
- y 850–1000: runway in perspective (VP centre of 10K), dashed centreline, edge lines, ~45 headlamp dots streaming toward the horizon, sticker "400 spots" (ember circle, ink text, -8° rotation) at right; left caption "Run into the dark." + "Headlamps provided."
- y 960–1080: date "FRI 22 MAY · 21:00" cream, Big Shoulders 900.
- y 1095–1200: two cells with photo tiles (track legs -> "Free, untimed group run"; dance floor -> "Afterparty at the store, DJ").
- y 1215–1290: CTA bar (ember) "Sign up · link in bio" + "stride.berlin".

Exact copy (en, US spelling): STRIDE COLLECTIVE · Berlin · Kreuzberg / NIGHT 10K / Run into the dark. Headlamps provided. / 400 spots / FRI 22 MAY · 21:00 / Free, untimed group run on the Tempelhof runways. / Afterparty at the store with a DJ. / Sign up · link in bio · stride.berlin

## 8. Asset plan
- Hero: sunset with dark hills (Open Images "Sunset", ChatSunset by Ray Ashley) — dark lower half is the negative space for runway and info; upscaled 2x (smooth sky, hides softness under grain).
- Tile 1: track + running legs with puddle reflection ("Track and field athletics", Alvaro_Tapia) — duotoned, cropped square.
- Tile 2: afterparty disco ball ("Disco", Jason Eppink) — blurry by nature, used small as texture.
- Rejected: flashlight candidates (laser pointer with visible text, white product shots), airport candidates (daytime snapshots, planes), athletics hurdles (crowded/sponsor logos).
