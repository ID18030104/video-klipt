# s02 — Aux Jardins de Marcel — plan

## 1. Business & offer
Primeur indépendant, rue de Marseille (Lyon 7e), tenu par Marcel et sa fille depuis 1998. Prix de quartier, fruits de saison. Offre : -30 % sur tous les fruits d'été (pêches, abricots, melons, nectarines, prunes), samedi et dimanche matin, jusqu'à épuisement.

## 2. Objective / platform
Footfall this weekend. Instagram Stories/Reels + Facebook, 9:16. Rewards: instant clarity, sound off, a loop, saves/shares by neighbours.

## 3. Viewer
Lyon 7e resident, thumb scrolling on Friday evening, sound off, 1.5 s. Wants a good deal on summer fruit, fears missing the end of the season / arriving to empty crates. Stops for a giant number on ripe fruit.

## 4. Decisions
- **A. Tier B** (Everyday promo, 10 s, 4 shots + loop return). Dials: social-native/retail punch (not polished), upbeat, warm + urgent, conversion, ephemeral. Over-producing would look fake for a corner shop.
- **B. Narrative**: 19 Offer blast (offer frame 1 → product glory shots → conditions → where/when). Alt: 5 countdown, 4 reveal — rejected, offer must be readable in one second.
- **C. Attention**: 1 bold number at t=0 ("-30 %", fully readable frame 0) ; 11 numbered progress "1/5…5/5" on the fruit cards, plus 19 CTA with urgency ("jusqu'à épuisement", "samedi dès 8 h"). Loop back to hook frame (20).
- **D. Style**: 27 Retail punch x 28 Market-stall authentic (kraft paper, price-tag/stamp, real produce, warm light). Alt: Kinfolk (too quiet), Neo-grotesk poster (too cold).
- **E. Image treatment**: one grade — shadows pushed to the shop's bottle green, highlights warmed (SVG component-transfer split-tone), slight contrast, grain on top. Alt: natural clean, warm film.
- **F. Type**: Anton (condensed, numbers/names, retail punch) + Fraunces (soft serif, warmth, addresses, brand). Not used recently (no history).
- **G. Colour**: photo-derived + brand-led: bottle green #0E2B1E (grocer's awning), cream #FFF1D6, apricot orange #FF8F2E, kraft #E4C48C, stamp red #E5462B.
- **H. Camera**: push-in on hero, snap-zoom pulls on the fruit cards, static tableau + elements on kraft.
- **I. Transitions**: main = hard cut on the beat (fruit cards); accents: badge iris (the -30 % sticker grows into the kraft screen), push with parallax + motion blur (kraft → end card), flash cut for the loop.
- **J. Format**: promo/offer spot 1080x1920, 10 s, 30 fps.

## 5. One message / one action
-30 % on all summer fruit this weekend, till stock runs out. Action: come to 42 rue de Marseille from Saturday 8 h.

## 6. Concept
The price tag is the hero: a giant "-30 %" lands on a real, sun-lit peach, then five fruit names cut past like crates being counted, and the sticker swells into the kraft paper of a market stall. Everything reads in one second and ends on the address — then loops back to the peach.
