## 6. Derived language
- Composition: each reason = one sheet of paper sliding in, a roller inks it top to bottom; image fills the upper 60%, number + words below; objects cropped by the sheet edge (rescale).
- Type: carved voice. Fraunces 900 (soft, cut-looking serif, heavy figures) for hooks and numerals; Familjen Grotesk 500/700 for the plain facts, like a printer's caption.
- Colour: one ink, deep olive-leaf green-black #22361A, on unbleached paper #EFE6CE (constraint). Paper shadow #D9CDAE for the stack only. No second hue.
- Material: SVG filter = ink voids + wood grain + rough edges; white gouge marks carved into every solid shape; paper fibre speckle.
- Motion: brayer roll = the print being pulled (reason to move); sheets stack like a print run; gouge strike on the lie.

## 7. Bold move
The 0.2% and the single olive printed so big they bleed off the sheet, in one ink only.
