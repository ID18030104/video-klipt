# o05 — Stride Collective "Night 10K" (post 1080x1350, EN)

**Business**: Kreuzberg running community + store. Free events build the community, the store sells the kit. Mid price, insider tone.
**Objective**: event attendance (400 sign-ups) on Instagram feed. Feed rewards a stop-and-read poster and saves/shares ("tag your running buddy").
**Viewer**: Berlin runner or curious beginner, 22–40, thumbing at night. Wants: a reason to go that isn't a race. Fears: being slow, being judged. Stopper: "Night 10K" huge + "no clock".
**Photo search**: Open Images has no night/runner photos (only daylight track, aircraft on runways); Pexels blocked. Decision: no photo — light and type build the image.
