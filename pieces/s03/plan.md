# s03 — Casa Almar, season opening (Instagram feed 4:5, EN)

## 1. Brief intelligence
1. **Business & offer**: 24-room boutique hotel in Comporta (dunes + rice fields, 1 h from Lisbon). Whitewashed cabanas, natural pool, restaurant cooking the day's catch over fire. Premium price level; differentiator is a quiet, barefoot, design-literate place, not a resort.
2. **Objective**: announcement + conversion (direct bookings). Runs in the Instagram feed (4:5). The feed rewards a strong single image, a readable idea at thumbnail size, and saves/shares from design-and-travel audiences.
3. **Viewer**: affluent couples 30-55, EU/US, following design and travel accounts. Thumb-scrolling, judging in 1.5 s by taste. Wants: a place that feels found, not sold. Fears: tourist-trap, over-branded resort. What stops them: a calm, beautiful frame and a date.
4. **Creative decisions**
   - **A. Tier S (flagship post)**, dials: polish/editorial (crafted, quiet), energy contemplative, register prestige/calm, funnel conversion (with a light touch), lifespan campaign (weeks until 15 May). Options: A / S / B. S chosen: premium hospitality, and the audience punishes anything loud.
   - **B. Reading order**: (1) arch photo, (2) huge date "15 May", (3) offer line, (4) CTA. Structure from catalogue B: Reveal-lite / Offer blast inverted to quiet luxury.
   - **C. Attention mechanic**: #1 bold number at t=0 (a giant "15" is the biggest element) + #24 exclusivity/perk (boat trip only for direct bookers). Options considered: question hook (too chatty), scarcity (not supplied, would be invented).
   - **D. Style**: Kinfolk / slow living x editorial serif (Kinfolk, Cereal, Aesop print): ivory paper, generous margins, small tracked caps, one arch-shaped photo window (whitewashed Alentejo arches). Options: Cinematic editorial, Quiet luxury, Kinfolk. Kinfolk chosen; quiet luxury felt too cold for a barefoot place.
   - **E. Treatment**: Warm film (Portra-like): lifted blacks, peach highlights, terracotta-tinted shadows, grain over everything (photo, paper, type). Overcast source sky is graded into a sunset glow.
   - **F. Type**: Instrument Serif (display, roman + italic) + Figtree (small caps labels, CTA). Soft serif + humanist sans.
   - **G. Colour**: earthy natural. Ivory #F1E9DA, sand #D9C7A6, terracotta #B4532F (taken from the schooner's own sails = photo-derived), estuary ink #1E2A2C.
   - **H. Camera**: static tableau (post).  **I. Transitions**: n/a (still).  **J. Format**: announcement card / editorial cover, 1080x1350.
5. **One message**: the summer season at Casa Almar opens 15 May. **One action**: book direct at casaalmar.pt (sunset boat trip on the Sado included).
6. **Concept**: A single arched window, like a whitewashed doorway, opens onto the estuary where a terracotta-sailed boat catches the last light; the date is set as monumental serif numerals beside it, like a page in a slow-living magazine. The perk (the sunset boat) is literally the picture, so the offer is shown before it is read.

## 7. Layout (1080x1350, 60 px margins)
- Header row (y 64): "CASA ALMAR" left, "COMPORTA · PORTUGAL" right, tracked caps 28 px, hairline rule.
- Arch photo left (x 60-620, y 150-910), 560 x 760, offset hairline arch outline in terracotta.
- Right column (x 660-1020): "Summer opens" (serif italic 62 px), giant "15" (serif, terracotta, ~380 px), "May" (serif italic 150 px).
- Bottom block: rule, statement in serif 58 px, CTA pill "Book at casaalmar.pt", small caps descriptor "24 rooms · dunes & rice fields".
