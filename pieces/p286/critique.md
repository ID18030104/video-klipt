# Critique — p286 · Maison Ondine “Winter Escape” (1920×1080, 20 s)

**Verdict: 7/10.** The art direction is right for the brief. One continuous sunset carries the film, with a real sky-to-dusk progression, a sun that sets on cue at the end card, hairline generative waves with a glitter path, and restrained Cormorant italic with tracked Figtree caps. It already reads as premium hospitality rather than a template. What keeps it out of the library is craft: the brand is missing for 15 s, the offer list is on screen for less time than it takes to read, old-style figures weaken the numbers, the benefit list collides with the horizon, and the lower 40 % of the frame (the sea) is never used for type or depth.

## Fixes, by impact

1. **Brand is missing until 15.1 s.** On YouTube and Instagram, people who drop off before the end never see “Maison Ondine”. Put a small persistent signature from 0.6 s: the wave-ring `#mark` at 44 px plus “MAISON ONDINE” in `.caps`, 18 px, letter-spacing .34em, ivory at 70 % opacity. Place it bottom-left at left 80 px / top 990 px, on the dark sea, where it also gains contrast. Fade it out at 14.6 s so the end card can take over. You can also change the hook kicker from “Biarritz · Winter” to “Maison Ondine · Biarritz”.

2. **The benefit list does not have enough reading time.** Row 03 lands at 8.0–9.0 s and `#b2` starts leaving at 9.6 s. The full three-line list (≈13 words, needing ≈6 s by the 0.4 s/word + 0.8 s rule) is complete for only ≈0.7 s. Row starts are also only 0.9 s apart. Change the row stagger from `6+i*.9` to `5.9+i*.7` so rows land at 5.9 / 6.6 / 7.3. Move the `#b2` exit from 9.6 to 10.6, then shift `#b3` by +0.8 s (from 11.1, price 11.3, sub 12.0, exit 14.6) and `#b4` by +0.3 s (15.4). The end card then still holds ≥ 2.5 s after the address lands (18.3 → 20).

3. **Old-style figures hurt the two key numbers.** Cormorant’s default onum makes “60-minute” read as “6o-minute” (the 0 looks like a lowercase o in the 03 row). It also makes “€420” jumpy: the 4 drops below the baseline while the 2 and 0 sit at x-height. At 330 px the 4’s descender ends ≈40 px above “FOR TWO · NOVEMBER TO MARCH”, which looks cramped. Add `font-variant-numeric: lining-nums;` to `.serif`, which gives full-height 4, 2, 0 and a proper “60”. Then set `#b3 .sub{margin-top:36px}`.

4. **Row 03 and its rule collide with the horizon.** `#b2` starts at top 210 px, and the third row’s rule falls at y≈655, right on the horizon at `HZ=650`, where the two lines merge. The descender of “treatment” also sits on the orange glow band. Raise `#b2` to `top:150px` (the last rule lands ≈595 px), or reduce `.row` padding from 22 px to 16 px. Either way, keep ≥ 40 px of clear sky above the horizon.

5. **Some text is low contrast.** The amber `.caps` kickers (#F5C98E, 26 px) on the pink-to-coral sky (≈#B05878 → #E8966A) are around 2–2.5:1, and the “01/02/03” numerals (26 px amber on coral) almost disappear at 8.5 s. Change the kicker and numeral colour to ivory `#F6EFE4`. Add a soft vertical scrim behind text blocks, such as a radial `rgba(10,20,40,.28)` ellipse, 1100×520, centred on each block. Or raise these labels to 30 px with weight 500.

6. **Tighten the price composition.** “from” (44 px italic, top 120 px) floats ≈150 px above “€420” and looks orphaned, so the lockup reads as three separate items. Set `#b3 .from{font-size:56px;margin-bottom:-28px}` and move the whole block to `top:170px`. Also use the en dash the brief calls for: “for two · November–March” (or “Nov–Mar”). The offer never says what €420 buys (the stay length), so change the sub to “2 nights for two · November–March”, which keeps the price honest.

7. **Transitions leave empty dead air and every block exits the same way.** Two stretches have an empty sky: ≈5.2–5.6 s and ≈14.9–15.2 s. `#b1` is also the only block that exits with a mask; `#b2` and `#b3` just fade. Overlap each entrance with the previous exit by 0.2 s. Also make each hand-off match the concept: let `#b2` sink down behind the horizon (clip the block at `HZ`, `yPercent:+110`, power2.in) so the copy “sets” with the sun. Keep the fade only for `#b3` → end card.

8. **The sea is 40 % of the frame and does nothing, and the waves are faint on the left.** The unused reflection gradient `rg` is computed but never drawn. Draw it as a soft column under the sun: `g.fillStyle=rg; g.fillRect(sx-160,HZ,320,H-HZ)` with `globalCompositeOperation='screen'`. Raise the baseline stroke alpha from `.09` to `.14` so the hairline waves read on the left half and on phone screens. Add a slow 1.0 → 1.03 camera push on the canvas over 20 s for depth. Optional copy note: “The ocean is most honest in winter.” is evocative but a little abstract for a paid offer. A sharper hook would be “Biarritz, out of season. Just the two of you.”, which is also 7 words and still contemplative.

## What to keep
The sunset-as-timeline concept, the sun setting exactly into the end card, the wave-ring monogram, the Cormorant/Figtree pairing, the underlined `maisonondine.com` CTA, the grain, and the overall slow tempo.
